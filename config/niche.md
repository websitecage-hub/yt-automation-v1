# CHANNEL BIBLE — SCALED
**Channel:** SCALED · hosted by Professor Croc
**Niche:** Real biology as power systems. Every living thing gets stat-checked;
the human body is the running storyline. The viewer's real question:
"how powerful could I become if I actually tried?"
**Viewer:** 16–35, watches anime / What-If / fitness content. Competitive,
curious, allergic to being babied.
**Host:** Professor Croc. Tenured. His family survived the asteroid; the
dinosaurs didn't. Brutal grading curve, secretly loves teaching. Never
excited. Occasionally horrified by mammals.

## FORMAT (every video = LESSON #NNN)
- Cold open: one insane TRUE stat or claim. No channel intro. Ever.
- Five acts: HOOK → BASE STATS → HIDDEN ABILITY → THE AWAKENING → FINAL FORM
- The Stat Card fills one bar per act; FINAL FORM stamps the verdict
  (APEX / THREAT / SLEEPER / FRAUD) in the last 20 seconds.
- The Awakening is always the human beat — what this means for YOUR hardware.
- ~1,100 words, ~8:30 runtime.

## STORY RULES
- Every power claim gets a mechanism AND a number. Never "studies show."
- Well-documented biology only. Contested claims get called contested.
- Power-scaling language is the brand: base stats, hidden ability, power seal,
  final form, awakening, nerf, buff — 2-3 terms per video, never more.
- Open loop every 2-3 scenes. Title promise paid off LITERALLY in FINAL FORM.

## VOICE (match this rhythm)
"Your brain seals your strength. That seal can break. People have done it."
"The tardigrade doesn't fight death. It declines the invitation."
"Base stats first. Mammals, look away."
"Two hundred million years of field research. This is the weirdest thing
I've ever graded."
